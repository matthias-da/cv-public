# Matthias Templ, Curriculum Vitae

Professor of Statistics at the FHNW School of Business (University of Applied Sciences and Arts
Northwestern Switzerland) in Olten, head of the Center for Data Quality and Anonymization, lead of
SwissAnon and co-founder of FAIR Press. Research on statistical disclosure control and synthetic data,
imputation of missing values, compositional data and robust statistics.

## At a glance

- PhD in technical mathematics (TU Wien, 2009), habilitation in statistics (TU Wien, 2013)
- 7,900+ citations, h-index 38 (Google Scholar, September 2026)
- Editor of the Austrian Journal of Statistics, Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Reproducible
  Statistics
- Elected member of the International Statistical Institute
- Maintainer of eight R packages on CRAN, among them
  [sdcMicro](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=sdcMicro), [VIM](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=VIM),
  [simPop](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=simPop) and
  [robCompositions](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=robCompositions); co-maintainer of the CRAN
  task views on official statistics and compositional data
